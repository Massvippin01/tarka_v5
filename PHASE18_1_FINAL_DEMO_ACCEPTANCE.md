## 3. LIVE DEMONSTRATION CONTINGENCY & RECOVERY PROCEDURES

| Failure Scenario | Immediate Detection | Remediation / Recovery Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Browser tab accidentally closed or refreshed** | Blank screen or redirect | Navigate back to `http://localhost:8080` (or `http://localhost:5173`). The UI automatically hydrates case state from `localStorage` and `useCaseStore` within 200ms. |
| **Upload connection timeout or stalled stream** | Progress bar pauses for > 5 seconds | Ingestion jobs are resumable: re-click "Resume Ingestion" or re-drop file; the system checks existing chunk offsets and resumes without re-uploading completed chunks. |
| **Backend or database service restarts** | Toast alert: `Reconnecting to telemetry stream...` | NGINX automatically reconnects to `/api/v1/health` with keepalive; the frontend EventSource client automatically retries with exponential backoff. |
| **Audio synthesis delay in voice mode** | Silence after clicking microphone | The UI gracefully falls back to deterministic on-screen text transcripts without blocking investigative actions. |
