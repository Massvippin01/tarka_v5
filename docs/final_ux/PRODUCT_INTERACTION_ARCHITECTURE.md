# TARKA | Product Interaction Architecture & Unified Experience Model

## Executive Summary

TARKA (तर्क) is an evidence-centred cyber-fraud investigation and attribution workbench engineered under the SUTRAM 2026 PS04 doctrine:
```
AI PROPOSES.
EVIDENCE VERIFIES.
HUMAN DECIDES.
```

The application provides a voice-first, particle-guided forensic intelligence environment designed for law enforcement cyber cells, financial intelligence units (FIUs), and judicial authorities. Rather than forcing investigators through rigid enterprise menus or exposing unstructured AI chat surfaces, TARKA unites autonomous voice navigation, real-time audio-reactive particle intelligence, and tactile skeuomorphic command consoles into a singular cohesive workflow.

## 2. Interaction Modes & State Transitions

### 2.1 Mode 1: Pure Particle Intelligence Canvas (`/`)
- **Visual Design**: Strict zero-clutter rule. No sidebars, no top headers, no conventional chatbot panes, no visible mic or send icons.
- **Entry Experience**: The investigator is greeted by the central 8,800-point 3D Fibonacci sphere in porcelain ivory and neural sapphire.
- **Audio Pipeline**: Real Web Audio API `AudioContext` + `AnalyserNode` with Voice Activity Detection (VAD).
- **Navigation Execution**: Saying *"Show me the evidence locker"* or *"Open the money trail"* navigates instantly while preserving case context.

### 2.2 Mode 2: Tactile Command Centre (`/?view=workspace`)
- **Visual Design**: Inspired by physical forensic flight instruments. Soft drop shadows, bevelled inset wells, warm parchment tones (`#E9E2D7` / `#F8F4ED`), and debossed typography.
- **Navigation**: Curved sidebar rail with tactile toggle. Quick switching across 22 operational forensic views.
- **Status HUD**: Real-time statutory compliance badge, cryptographic hash verification ticker, and resource admission metrics.

### 2.3 Mode 3: Contextual Guided Investigation
- At any point on any screen, pressing `?` or `G` launches the **Continuous Forensic Guide Modal**.
- The modal reveals:
  1. **Investigator Operational Narrative**: What this screen proves, how to interpret metrics, statutory legal relevance under BNSS §94 & §107.
  2. **Technical Algorithmic Deep-Dive**: Underlying complexity, data structures (e.g., Louvain community clustering, BFS flow conservation), and latency guarantees.
  3. **Decoded Abbreviations**: Instant glossary (RRN, CGNAT, IMEI, IMSI, BSA, BNSS).
  4. **Next Recommended Actions**: Direct clickable transitions to dependent workflows.

## 3. Strict Safety & Governance Principles

1. **Non-Invasive AI Proposals**: The assistant never commits judicial actions, freezes bank accounts, or modifies ledger records autonomously. It prepares actions in the *Pending Judicial Review* queue.
2. **Deterministic Cryptographic Verification**: Every claim made by the assistant is grounded in an immutable SHA-256 artifact hash and Section 63 Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) digital certificate.
3. **Fail-Closed Offline Operation**: When internet connectivity is severed, the system defaults to 100% local processing with complete zero-TTS silence (visual responses only) to ensure no unverified audio generation occurs.
