# SENTINELX NEXUS — Investigative Workflows & Synchronization Fabric

## Core Investigative Surface
The persistent **Investigation Surface** provides four synchronized dimensions: Case, Time, Evidence, and Analysis.

```
FRAGMENTED RECORDS
        ↓
INGEST & NORMALIZE
        ↓
ENTITY RESOLUTION (Non-destructive candidate match)
        ↓
CONNECT & CORRELATE (Bounded Multi-Hop Projection)
        ↓
BIDIRECTIONAL SYNCHRONIZATION (Graph ↔ Map ↔ Timeline ↔ Evidence ↔ Copilot)
        ↓
EXPLAIN (Primary Novelty #1: Relationship Inspector WHY? breakdown)
        ↓
CHALLENGE (Primary Novelty #2: Evidence Gap & Contradiction Inspector)
        ↓
ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESIS (H1 / H2 / H3 Matrix)
        ↓
NEXT VERIFICATION (Recommended Human Investigator Action)
        ↓
HUMAN INVESTIGATOR DECISION
```
