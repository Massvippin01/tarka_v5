## 1. THE NATIONAL CYBER-FRAUD CRISIS & THE INVESTIGATIVE TRILEMMA

India's digital economy handles over 14 billion UPI transactions every month. Alongside this unprecedented financial democratization, organized cybercrime syndicates have weaponized digital arrest lures, APK overlay trojans, mule banking rings, and instant cross-bank cashouts.

Traditional forensic solutions fail because of an unaddressed **investigative trilemma**:
1. **Multi-Institutional Fragmentation:** Siphoned funds traverse 4–7 banking and payment layers within minutes, but banks operate in silos and cannot share raw customer PII without violating the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023.
2. **Spatio-Temporal & Attribution Paradoxes:** Telecom IPDR/CGNAT records and Core Banking timestamps suffer from unsynchronized NTP drifts and ambiguous alibis, leading to false merges or dropped prosecutions.
3. **Judicial Inadmissibility:** Machine-learning heuristics generate probabilistic "black-box" risk scores that fail Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023.
