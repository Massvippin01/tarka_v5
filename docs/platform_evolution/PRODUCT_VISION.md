## 2. Core Pillars of Forensic Excellence

### 2.1 The Inviolability of Raw Evidence (`DATA_ONLY`)
Evidence admitted into TARKA is strictly immutable. Binary bitstreams are sealed with whole-object SHA-256 hashes and 64KB chunk manifests. Raw content is never interpreted as executable code, and prompt injection attempts embedded within suspect files remain passive observational data.

### 2.2 Epistemic Humility & Contradiction Resolution
TARKA never declares a suspect guilty or innocent. Instead, the deterministic Z3 SMT solver isolates Minimal Unsatisfiable Subsets (MUS) to detect temporal, geographic, and financial contradictions across independent exhibits (e.g., ATM withdrawals occurring simultaneously with active cellular connections 400 km away).

### 2.3 Indigenous Language Naturalness
Investigating officers operating across Indian jurisdictions converse in Indian English, Tamil, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, and Gujarati, including code-mixed dialects (Tanglish/Hinglish). Legal terms and Indian currency denominations (Lakhs, Crores) are pronounced natively without robotic distortion.
