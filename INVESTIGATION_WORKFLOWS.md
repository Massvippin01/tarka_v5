# SENTINELX NEXUS — Investigative Workflows & Epistemic Separation

## Core Investigative Surface
The persistent **Investigation Surface** provides four core dimensions: Case, Time, Evidence, and Analysis.

```
FRAGMENTED RECORDS
        ↓
INGEST & NORMALIZE
        ↓
ENTITY RESOLUTION (Non-destructive candidate match)
        ↓
CONNECT & CORRELATE (Multi-hop path analysis)
        ↓
EXPLAIN (Primary Novelty #1: Relationship Inspector WHY? breakdown)
        ↓
CHALLENGE (Primary Novelty #2: Evidence Gap & Contradiction Inspector)
        ↓
ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESIS (H1 / H2 / H3 Matrix)
        ↓
NEXT VERIFICATION (Recommended Human Investigator Action)
        ↓
HUMAN INVESTIGATOR DECISION
```

## Epistemic Separation Guarantee
- `OBSERVATION` ≠ `INFERENCE` ≠ `HYPOTHESIS` ≠ `DECISION`
- Inference confidence is never presented as criminal guilt.
- Every analytical claim exposes supporting evidence, contradicting observations, evidence gaps, and next verification steps.
