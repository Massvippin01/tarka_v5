## 1. Evaluation Objectives

The collaborative evaluation between the **Host Institution** and the **TARKA × SentinelX Engineering Consortium** is established to achieve the following specific, non-commercial objectives:
1. **Algorithmic Validation:** Evaluate the accuracy, latency, and false-alarm boundaries of TARKA’s TRACE tri-forensic raster suite (Error Level Analysis, 2D-FFT Radon resampling transforms, and micro-typography baselines) against standardized payment evidence.
2. **Reconciliation & Throughput Testing:** Validate high-speed Core Banking System (CBS) balance conservation algebra and fuzzy 12-digit UTR reference cross-matching under institutional transaction volumes.
3. **Statutory Compliance Audit:** Verify whether automated reports generated by TARKA fulfill the statutory and evidentiary requirements of **Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)**.
4. **Workflow Optimization:** Identify operational friction points in digital payment evidence triage to inform future software releases.

## 4. Data Ownership, Custody & Confidentiality

1. **Absolute Partner Ownership:** The Host Institution retains exclusive, unencumbered ownership of all data, evidence exhibits, examiner notes, case logs, and outputs processed during the evaluation.
2. **Zero Ingress / Egress:** TARKA operates in a self-contained, air-gapped environment. The software contains **zero telemetry, cloud phone-home routines, or third-party tracking APIs**.
3. **Confidentiality:** Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding proprietary evaluation findings, institutional operational workflows, and unreleased software architectures.

---

## 5. Data Retention, Quarantine & Certified Deletion

1. **Local Storage Only:** All ingested artifacts are stored strictly in local Content-Addressed Storage (`data/evidence_cas/`) and local SQLite/PostgreSQL instances on the Host Institution's designated workstation.
2. **Trial Conclusion Deletion:** Upon conclusion or termination of the evaluation, the Host Institution may execute a single certified wipe command (`python scripts/purge_evaluation_data.py`), which securely unlinks and overwrites all case directories, database rows, and temporary caches.

---

## 6. Information Security & Air-Gapping Controls

1. **Offline Air-Gap Integrity:** TARKA must be capable of completing all forensic raster evaluations, OCR, CBS reconciliations, and Z3 SMT proofs without any active internet connection.
2. **Financial PII Masking:** Under Section 8 of the DPDP Act, 2023, the Multimodal Evidence Gateway automatically detects and masks Aadhaar numbers (first 8 digits redacted per UIDAI standards), Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), and third-party bank account numbers prior to indexing.
3. **Polyglot & Binary Threat Rejection:** The software enforces magic-byte validation, blocking nested executable binaries (PE/ELF), script injection vectors, and decompression archive bombs.

## 7. Independent Evaluation & Objective Reporting

1. **Independent Discretion:** The Host Institution retains complete scientific and editorial independence in assessing TARKA's performance, false-positive rates, and limitations.
2. **Transparent Error Disclosure:** The Consortium guarantees that all known limitations (including ELA variance on uniform WhatsApp compression and OCR character ambiguities) are fully disclosed prior to evaluation.

## 10. Term, Suspension & Termination

1. **Evaluation Term:** The standard evaluation term shall be **thirty (30) calendar days** from the date of software installation, extendable by mutual written agreement.
2. **Termination at Will:** Either party may terminate the evaluation at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon three (3) business days' written notice.
3. **Post-Termination Obligations:** Upon termination, all evaluation licenses expire immediately, and the certified data deletion protocol set forth in Section 5 shall be executed.
