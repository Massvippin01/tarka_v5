# SentinelX --- World-Class UI/UX & Interaction Specification

## SIH 2026 · PS 26189 · Celestials

**Status: Design-lock candidate / implementation authority**

SentinelX is one coherent investigative intelligence operating system,
not a collection of dashboards or mockups.

**Product north star:** From fragmented records → connected evidence →
explainable investigative intelligence.

**Operating loop:** DISCOVER → CONNECT → VERIFY → EXPLAIN → DECIDE

**AI principle:** ML PROPOSES → EVIDENCE VERIFIES → HUMAN DECIDES

This specification locks the product philosophy, visual system,
application shell, navigation, investigation workspace, voice assistant,
responsive behavior, interaction contracts, evidence/trust UX,
technology architecture, screen behavior and engineering acceptance
tests.

### Absolute prohibitions

No generic dashboard aesthetic, disconnected mockups, fake buttons, dead
routes, cyberpunk/neon/glassmorphism, decorative gradients, excessive
animation, icon-only critical controls, opaque AI claims,
guilt-by-association UI, autonomous investigative decisions, fabricated
government integrations, or fixed-layout dependence.

### Quality bar

The result must feel like a mature government/enterprise intelligence
workstation that a new investigator can learn quickly while an
experienced officer can operate at high speed.
