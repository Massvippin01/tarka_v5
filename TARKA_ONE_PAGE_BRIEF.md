## Sovereign Multimodal Payment Forensics & Statutory Evidence Platform

### The Investigative Crisis
Indian law enforcement and banking fraud risk units are confronting an unprecedented surge in digital payment manipulation. Every day, thousands of fabricated UPI payment confirmation screenshots (generated via "FakePay" APKs or image editing tools) are presented to merchants, victims, and investigating officers. Investigating Officers spend days waiting for bank branch confirmations, while Forensic Science Laboratories face multi-month backlogs performing manual, unstandardized visual inspections.

### What is TARKA?
**TARKA** is an indigenous, high-throughput digital payment forensic and investigation platform built specifically for Indian financial rails (UPI, IMPS, RTGS, NEFT). It fuses deterministic raster forensics, automated Core Banking System (CBS) ledger reconciliation, and formal mathematical reasoning to empower investigators to dissect questioned transactions in seconds—producing court-admissible evidence certificates under the new criminal laws.

### Strict Statutory & Security Safeguards

- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (Section 63):** Fully replaces legacy Section 65B requirements with cryptographically verified device particulars, algorithm baselines, and substantive SHA-256 fingerprints.
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:** On-premises, zero-telemetry architecture with built-in financial PII masking (Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers).
- **ISO/IEC 27037:2012 Custody Invariant:** Streaming SHA-256 hashing at ingestion into immutable Content-Addressed Storage (CAS); append-only parent-hash chained audit ledger.
- **The Non-Promotable Invariant:** The system strictly defaults all findings to `human_verified = FALSE`. Algorithms never classify guilt or innocence; the human investigator retains absolute statutory decision-making authority.
